## Available actions {#actions}

CloakView plans global shortcuts for **quick hide** (toggle the private hub), **push-to-ask** (show the Ask window), and **start/stop listen**. You can use them while another app is focused. These controls do not join or modify your call.

## Configure your keys {#configure}

Open **CloakView → Settings → Shortcuts** and review the displayed assignments before relying on them in a meeting. The quick-hide default is Command–Shift–H. Choose combinations that do not conflict with your meeting app or macOS. Save changes and test each action in a harmless window.

## If a shortcut does nothing {#conflicts}

Try its button in the app first. Check whether macOS or another app already uses the combination; change it in CloakView Settings or the other app. If the shortcut starts listening, check capture and microphone permissions, plus your API key, before testing again. A hidden hub is not proof that capture stopped: use **Stop Listen** to end capture.
